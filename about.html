What's in My Toolkit

Professional Experience

Joined as Gokomodo's first and only designer. No team, no process, no design system. Built everything from scratch. Over four years, grew into a leadership role while simultaneously growing the team from 1 to 8 across product designers and graphic designers. Led design across five product verticals, including a loyalty platform that has grown to 60,000+ members.

I collaborated closely with Project Managers and Developer teams to create a diverse range of cross-media projects, focusing on UI/UX design for B2C, B2B companies, and government entities. My process involved conducting thorough user research to understand client goals, creating both low-fidelity and high-fidelity designs, and conducting testing to ensure the final results align seamlessly with client objectives.

I actively engage with business users and project managers to enhance operational efficiency and effectiveness. I create actionable insights for dashboard field campaign monitoring, problem reports, and warranty products for heavy equipment units across both web and mobile platforms.
